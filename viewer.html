Product Architecture

Pillars, UX rules, user journeys, engagement MVP

End-to-End User Journey

  1. Sign up + Onboarding — 3-step flow with seed insight
  2. Citadel — Connect Plaid, QBO, payroll
  3. Vault — Upload tax documents
  4. Logos — AI chat surfaces tax position
  5. Enchiridion — Strategy playbook with savings
  6. CPA Marketplace — Engagement request with context
  7. CPA implements — Strategies filed, Ledger updated
  8. Ledger — Portable history across CPAs
